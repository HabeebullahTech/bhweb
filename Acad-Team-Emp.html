About the academy

Structured Islamic education, delivered online

BaytulHub Online Academy, operating under Al-Hirth Institute, teaches students locally and internationally. Our aim isn't students who simply know more — it's students who understand, practise, and carry good character into their families and communities.

  • Qur'an memorisation & revision
  • Recitation & Tajweed
  • Arabic language
  • Islamic studies
  • Hajj & Umrah education
  • Youth & children's education
  • Mentorship & development
  • Counselling & family support

01Qur'an & Hifdh Tutors+

Support students in memorisation, revision, recitation, correction, Tajweed and steady academic progress. Open to male and female applicants.

What the role involves

  • Conducting scheduled Qur'an classes
  • Listening to and correcting memorisation
  • Supervising daily and weekly revision
  • Monitoring progress and flagging gaps
  • Keeping accurate student records
  • Communicating with parents where needed

What we're looking for

  • Strong recitation and sound Tajweed
  • Memorisation appropriate to the role
  • Patience and clear communication
  • Punctuality and consistency
  • Relevant ijazah or teaching experience is a plus
02Arabic Language Tutors+

Build students' practical and academic Arabic, according to the programme you're assigned. Open to male and female applicants.

What the role involves

  • Teaching vocabulary, grammar and structure
  • Developing reading, comprehension and speech
  • Preparing lessons and materials
  • Running assessments and giving feedback
  • Supporting Qur'an comprehension

What we're looking for

  • Solid Arabic proficiency, Fusha preferred
  • Relevant academic or teaching background
  • Ability to explain clearly to beginners
  • Willingness to join curriculum development

What the role involves

  • Assisting exams, assessments and reports
  • Coordinating academic activities

What we're looking for

  • Organised, observant, dependable
  • Comfortable in digital systems

  • Sound judgement on student welfare

Working online

What the role and the environment ask of you

The online environment

Set up to teach and work well

  • A stable internet connection for classes and meetings
  • A computer, tablet or phone suited to your role
  • A quiet, professional space during official activities
  • Willingness to learn the digital tools your role needs
  • Availability at agreed times, with prompt notice if that changes
General requirements

What most positions expect

  • Relevant qualifications, training or demonstrable experience
  • Clear, respectful communication
  • Punctuality, organisation and dependability
  • Comfort working in an online environment
  • Confidentiality and good character
  • Willingness to follow academy policies and keep developing
For Qur'an & Islamic education roles

Teaching Islamic knowledge takes particular care

We value teachers who bring this alongside their subject knowledge:

  • Respect for the Qur'an and Sunnah, and teaching from sound principles
  • Good adab, and honesty about the limits of one's own knowledge
  • Willingness to refer difficult matters to qualified scholars
  • Patience with students who differ in age, ability and pace
  • A focus on character and practice, not only academic knowledge
How it works

The recruitment process

1

Application

Complete the BaytulHub Staff Application Form with the required information.

2

Screening

Your application is reviewed against the requirements of the role you applied for.

3

Interview

Shortlisted applicants are invited to an online or in-person interview, where applicable.

4

Assessment

A teaching demonstration, recitation, language or technical assessment, depending on the role.

5

Verification

Successful applicants receive details on their role, working arrangements and orientation.
