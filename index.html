From knowledge to a life that is lived

Many of us learn about the Qur'an without ever feeling it change our daily lives. BaytulHub exists to close that gap, walking with individuals and families through a clear progression rather than leaving them with information alone.

BaytulHub applies the principles of fiqh al-mu'amalat, the Islamic guidance on transactions and dealings, to every service we offer. They shape how a class is scheduled, how a counselling session is booked and how a technology project is delivered.

BaytulHub Online Academy

A Qur'an journey designed to go beyond memorization

Our most important programme is Qur'an memorization, and it carries a complete system around it: structured curriculum, qualified teachers, one-on-one correction, tracked progress and a Muroja'ah system that keeps the Qur'an alive in a student's life long after a page is first memorized.

What every student receives

  • Structured curriculum with qualified teachers
  • One-on-one correction of recitation
  • Flexible online learning across time zones
  • Memorization and progress tracking
  • Islamic character development woven into lessons
  • Regular progress reports for parents
  • Islamic Studies and Arabic alongside memorization

The two-juz principle

Every Academy student, whether or not they are enrolled in the memorization programme, is expected to memorize Juz 29 and Juz 30. A Muslim cannot worship Allah properly without knowing Him, so this includes tafsir, understanding the surahs, the names of Allah mentioned in them, and how to apply their lessons, so that every student can use the Qur'an in salah and live by what they learn.

Core differentiator

Muroja'ah: the soul of memorization

Memorization is not about accumulating pages. Muroja'ah, the ongoing revision of what has been memorized, is what turns memorization into a sustainable relationship with the Qur'an.

  • At least 2 ajzaa revised daily
  • Assessment every 3 days
  • App reminders
  • Dedicated accountability partner
  • Two check-ins a day by message or call
  • Alert if Muroja'ah is missed 2 days running
