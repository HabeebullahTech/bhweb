You wanted to learn. The fees said no.

Many sincere people never start, and the reason is rarely a lack of intention.

Fees came first

A good program costs money that a student, an orphan's household or a young family may not have.

Started, then stopped

Without teachers, a schedule and revision, memorization fades within weeks.

Left to carry it alone

Applications received so far

Who can apply, and what each place offers

Each category is a different stage of life with its own requirements. Read yours before you apply.

What every scholar commits to

The scholarship is free to receive, but it is not free of effort.

One hour every day

Classes, memorization, murajaah, tajweed and assignments. This is the minimum, not the maximum.

Memorize and revise

New portions plus regular revision, recited to your teacher until it is strong.

Truthful information

Any false or altered information means immediate disqualification, before or after the award.

Reviewed each term

Places are reviewed every term or semester for attendance, progress, conduct and continued need.

How selection works

Meeting the requirements does not guarantee a place. Every application is reviewed on eligibility, financial need, commitment and the information provided.

Apply by 10 October

Fill in the form, upload one supporting document and name a referee. You receive a reference by email.

We review

The team checks eligibility, circumstances and referees. Your documents are private and deleted after 10 days.

Results on 25 October
